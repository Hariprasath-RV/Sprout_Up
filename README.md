# Overview

SproutUP is a smart agriculture platform developed to assist farmers in making data-driven decisions through intelligent crop price prediction and market analysis. The application provides personalized market insights, government scheme information, multilingual support, and a farmer reward system through an interactive and user-friendly dashboard.

The platform is designed to improve agricultural decision-making by providing timely information that helps farmers maximize profitability while simplifying access to agricultural resources.

---------------------------------------------------------

# Problem Statement

Farmers often face uncertainty regarding market prices, crop demand, and the best time to sell their produce. Limited access to real-time agricultural information and government welfare programs affects profitability and decision-making.

SproutUP addresses these challenges by providing a centralized platform that delivers personalized crop insights, market trends, and government support information.

----------------------------------------------------------

# Objectives

* Provide intelligent crop price prediction.
